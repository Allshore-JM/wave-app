This is a modeled estimate with known uncertainty (~35–80 m), prior-pinned to a single surfer observation and not independently validated. It is not a safety device.

1. Method & data provenance

  • Bathymetry: NOAA NCEI CUDEM 1/9″ (~3 m) tile ncei19_n21x75_w158x25_2021v1.tif, vertical datum LMSL.
  • Constraint check: per-tile source-coverage polygons + 2013 USACE NCMP topobathy lidar (dataset 5034) point returns.
  • Breaking model: Komar–Gaughan breaker height from deep-water Rayleigh set classes (H1/10, H1/100) × a spot refraction gain, breaking at a depth = γ·breaker-height band. The refraction gain is pinned so the biggest sets of a 10 ft @ 17 s swell break at 12.3 m (the observed onset) — prior calibration, not validation.

5. Accuracy & what this is not

  • MODELED ESTIMATE with known uncertainty -- not a safety device.
  • kr0 is a PINNED PRIOR (onset matches the surfer's observation), NOT an independent fit; reproducing the onset depth is prior calibration, not validation.
  • The '10 ft @ 17 s' onset is interpreted as buoy significant Hs (c_units=1.0); if it is Hawaiian scale the whole ladder shifts (~2x lever) -- unresolved.
  • Direction-shift s_dir=0 (prior): the peak is assumed on the fix line; bins far off the reference direction (315 deg) carry lower confidence until observations exist.
  • Modeled guidance radius ~35-80 m; the 30 ft target is display precision, not accuracy.
  • impact_zone_seaward_line is the SEAWARD boundary of the biggest sets' impact zone; danger is being SHOREWARD of it (danger_side). It is NOT a precise shoreward warning line -- that is deferred to the watch-app phase.

6. Do-not-rely-on

  • The γ=0.78-on-Hs flat-bottom rule (misplaces the break by 100+ m) — explicitly avoided; breaking is computed per wave class.
  • Whether ‘10 ft’ is buoy Hs or Hawaiian scale (a ~2× lever) — assumed buoy Hs; unresolved until logged observation.
  • Vertical-datum offsets for MHW/MLLW/geoid cross-checks are placeholders pending NOAA VDatum (the primary CUDEM surface is native LMSL, so the reef geometry is conversion-free).
  • Direction-driven alongshore peak shift (set to zero here) — off-NW bins carry lower confidence until observations exist.
