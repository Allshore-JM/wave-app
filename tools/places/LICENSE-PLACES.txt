Map borders and place names on allshoresurf.com (static/places/v1)
==================================================================

These files are derived from two sources. They were converted, cut into 10-degree cells, simplified (the world file
only), filtered and de-duplicated by Allshore Surf with tools/places/build_places.py in the wave-app repository.

1. Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)
   Files: ne_10m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land, ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces_lines, ne_10m_populated_places,
   from github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector at commit ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19.
   Natural Earth data is in the public domain; no permission is needed to use it. Credit: "Made with Natural Earth."
   Boundaries are shown as Natural Earth draws them and imply no position on any disputed border; lines Natural Earth
   marks as disputed, indefinite or lines of control are kept apart from settled borders.

2. GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/)
   File: cities1000.zip (towns with at least 1,000 inhabitants) from download.geonames.org/export/dump/; the date and
   sha256 of the copy used are recorded in index.json.
   GeoNames data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0):
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Attribution: GeoNames (www.geonames.org). Changes made: only populated places with the feature codes and population
   listed in index.json are kept, and of each only its name, position and population. Names are cut to 60 bytes, and
   names joining several areas with " / " are left out. A town is left out when it is a Natural Earth place: same
   GeoNames id; same name within 15 km; or one of its alternate names within 5 km. Of two towns with one name within
   2 km, the smaller is left out. At most 8,000 places are kept in each 10-degree cell, the largest first. Positions are
   rounded to 0.0001 degree. A rank and the zoom from which the map shows a town are derived from its population. The
   data is provided as is, without warranty.
